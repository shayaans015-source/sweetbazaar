Festival calendar

Which festival, and how much off

The shop runs a sale around every major festival and religious event. Discounts are set item by item between 5% and 15%, so nothing is ever a flat rate. Dates are loaded for 2025–2028.

Dates follow the Hindu lunisolar and Islamic calendars, so they shift every year — for example Diwali falls on 8 Nov in 2026 and 29 Oct in 2027. Each sale opens a couple of weeks before the festival and settles a day or two after.
